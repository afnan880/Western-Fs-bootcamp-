Afnan Mudassar

Afnan has amassed 14 years of professional squash experience, including a distinguished tenure as a national champion and international representative for Pakistan. Additionally, he has spent 2 years coaching in Pakistan, honing his skills and imparting knowledge. He holds a certification as an intermediate coach from Squash Canada and is a qualified Level 1 WSO referee.
